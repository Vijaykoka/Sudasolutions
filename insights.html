Data

Building Data-driven Organizations: Key Challenges and How to Overcome Them?

Every business wants to be data-driven — but few manage it. The blockers are rarely technical: they are silos, trust and culture. Start by consolidating your data estate, defining shared metrics and giving teams self-serve access through governed dashboards. Then treat analytics as a habit, not a project. With the right foundation in place, data stops being a report and becomes a competitive advantage.

Critical to Accelerate Digital Transformation Post-Pandemic

The pandemic proved that organisations which had already invested in digital capacity could pivot overnight — while those that hadn’t were left scrambling. Digital transformation is no longer "nice to have". It is the difference between surviving and thriving. Prioritise the processes that touch customers most, modernise incrementally and ship value continuously rather than waiting for one big-bang programme.

Security

Cyber Security — An Attacker's View Post-Pandemic

Since the pandemic, attackers have leaned into remote work: phishing, credential stuffing and business email compromise now dominate. The attacker’s view is simple — find the weakest human, not the strongest firewall. Defend accordingly: enforce multi-factor authentication everywhere, segment your network, patch relentlessly and run regular phishing tests. Security is a behaviour, not a product.
